🔔 Ring a meeting alarm

One click and the alarm rings at the same moment on every selected employee's mobile and computer, until they tap "I'm coming".

How it works

Employees need to have CyberAttend open (browser tab or phone) and tap once on the page to allow sound. The alarm shows full-screen, rings loudly, vibrates on phones and keeps ringing until the employee taps "I'm coming" or you stop it (auto-stops after {{ 15 }} minutes).

If SMS is set up, employees with a phone number also get a text message.
